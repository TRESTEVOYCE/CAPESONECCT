Getting Started with CAPESONNECT

Localized Digital Job Matching and Management System for the Municipality of Carigara

What is CAPESONNECT?

CAPESONNECT is a web-based management and decision-support system engineered specifically for the Public Employment Service Office (PESO) in Carigara, Leyte. Built to solve administrative bottlenecks, eliminate slow paper-based registrations, and overcome the limitations of rigid national databases, CAPESONNECT automates local job matching, applicant tracking, and DOLE program reporting.

How CAPESONNECT Operates (Core Modules & Workflow)

  1. Multi-Role User Ecosystem: Supports accounts for Applicants (Jobseekers), Employers (Private & Public), PESO Administrators, and partner agency roles (e.g., MSWDO).
  2. Digital Onboarding & Verification: Replaces manual paper forms. Jobseekers build standardized profiles with educational and skills data. Employers upload tax/business permits (e.g., COR 2303, DTI/SEC) or public appointment papers for PESO approval.
  3. Deterministic Rule-Based Matching Engine: Rather than simple keyword searches, CAPESONNECT uses multi-indicator Boolean logic to align jobseeker skills, education level, license eligibility, and disability/OFW inclusion flags directly with active job posting constraints.
  4. Referral & Placement Tracking: PESO officers can issue formal referrals (OfferedJobs) and monitor candidate statuses (Pending → Reviewed → For Interview → Hired).
  5. Special DOLE Program Registries: Enrolls, tracks, and manages beneficiaries for municipal and national programs including SPES, GIP, TUPAD, DILP, and Career Guidance.
  6. Automated Statistical Matrix Reporting: Generates monthly labor market information (LMI) reports instantly for DOLE submission, tracking local employment rates and demographic metrics without manual calculation errors.

Applicant & Employer Verification

Verifying Jobseeker Profiles

  1. Navigate to Applicant Registry on the sidebar.
  2. Filter by Pending status.
  3. Click the sequence ID (e.g., AP-0001) to inspect applicant demographics, education, and resume attachments.
  4. Click Approve to activate the profile for job referrals or Reject if information is invalid.

Employer Business Verification

Private establishments must attach photocopies of COR 2303, DTI/SEC registration, or Business Permits. Public government agencies submit CSC appointment papers or DBM Plantilla extracts. Review these uploads in the Employer List before granting posting rights.

Beneficiary Programs Management

Enrolling Program Beneficiaries

To enroll individuals under SPES, GIP, TUPAD, DILP, or Career Guidance:

  • Open Special Programs from the sidebar.
  • Select the target program tab and click + Enroll Beneficiary.
  • Provide daily salary rates, project duration dates, educational background, and save the form.

Account Security & Credentials

Step-by-Step Guide: Changing Your Registered Email

  1. Go to Account Settings from your profile menu.
  2. In the email field under profile credentials, type your new email address and click Request Email Verification Code.
  3. The system generates a secure 6-digit One-Time Password (OTP) and sends it directly to your new email inbox.
  4. An OTP verification modal will automatically appear on screen. Enter the 6-digit code.
  5. Click Verify Email OTP to finalize and save your new email address.

Step-by-Step Guide: Changing Your Password

  1. Navigate to Account Settings.
  2. Locate the Change Password panel and click Change Password to open the security form.
  3. Enter your Current Password, followed by your New Password and Confirm New Password.
  4. Click Send Security Code. The system dispatches a 6-digit OTP to your registered administrator email address.
  5. In the security verification modal, enter the 6-digit code and click Confirm Password Change. Your session will be updated securely.
