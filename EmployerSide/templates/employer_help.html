Portal Overview & Ecosystem

Understanding CAPESONNECT for Employers in Carigara, Leyte

What is CAPESONNECT Employer Portal?

It is the official digital recruitment platform managed by the Public Employment Service Office (PESO) in Carigara, Leyte, connecting local establishments directly with qualified jobseekers.

Employer Verification Requirements

To maintain platform security, employers must provide valid business documentation (such as DTI/SEC registration, business permits, or BIR COR 2303). Once reviewed and approved by PESO Admin, full publishing rights are unlocked.

Managing Jobs and Applicants

Step-by-step instructions for your daily workflow

How to Post and Update Job Vacancies

  1. Click on Jobs Posted in the sidebar navigation.
  2. Click Post New Job and fill out position details, salary, and requirements.
  3. To edit an existing listing, click the job title and select Edit Job to alter its status (Active, Filled, or Closed).

Reviewing Applicants

Navigate to the Applicants tab to view candidate submissions. Click on any applicant to review their resume, background profile, and update their recruitment stage.

Account Security & Credentials

Secure OTP verification workflows

Changing Your Email with OTP

  1. Go to Settings from your sidebar.
  2. Enter your new email address and click Request Verification Code.
  3. Enter the 6-digit OTP code sent to your inbox in the verification prompt to complete the update.
