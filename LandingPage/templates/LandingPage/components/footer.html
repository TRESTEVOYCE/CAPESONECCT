CAPESONECCT Privacy Policy & Notice

CAPESONECCT

CAPESONECCT ("the System") respects your right to privacy.

This Privacy Policy explains how CAPESONECCT collects, records, stores, uses, accesses, and otherwise processes personal information in connection with its employment, workforce, beneficiary, and administrative functions.

This Privacy Policy is intended to provide transparency regarding the System's processing of personal information and to explain the rights available to individuals whose information is processed.

CAPESONECCT is intended to operate in accordance with the Republic Act No. 10173, also known as the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (DPA), its Implementing Rules and Regulations, and applicable issuances of the National Privacy Commission (NPC).

The DPA requires personal-data processing to observe the principles of transparency, legitimate purpose, and proportionality.

2. Who Are the Data Subjects?

CAPESONECCT may process information belonging to several categories of individuals, including:

  • Applicants and jobseekers;
  • Employers and authorized employer representatives;
  • PESO personnel;
  • MSWDO personnel;
  • Program beneficiaries;
  • Career guidance participants;
  • System administrators;
  • Authorized government or institutional personnel; and
  • Other individuals whose information is legitimately submitted or processed through the System.

3. What Information Does CAPESONECCT Collect?

CAPESONECCT may collect different categories of personal information depending on the user's role and the services being used.

A. Account Information

When creating an account, the System may collect:

  • Full name or username;
  • Email address;
  • Password credentials in protected form;
  • Account role;
  • Account status;
  • Email-verification status;
  • Account creation and update timestamps;
  • Login-related information; and
  • Profile picture, when provided.

Passwords are not intended to be stored as readable/plain-text passwords.

4. Applicant / Jobseeker Information

An applicant profile may contain substantially more information than ordinary account registration. This may include:

Identity Information
  • First name, middle name, last name, suffix;
  • Date and place of birth, sex, civil status, nationality;
  • Applicant identification/reference number and image/document.
Contact Information
  • Primary/additional email, mobile numbers, landline number;
  • Residential address, street/house info, barangay, municipality, province, region, ZIP code.
Employment Information
  • Current employment status, reason for unemployment, job search duration, expected/preferred salary;
  • Willingness to work immediately, OFW status, intention to return to the Philippines, preferred occupations and locations.
Education Information
  • Educational attainment, school/university, course/program, year graduated, school status, history;
  • Awards, training hours/institutions, certifications, licenses or eligibility info.
Skills Information & Documents

Computer skills, driving, customer service, bookkeeping, carpentry, welding, resumes, CVs, and supporting identification documents.

5. Applicant Program and Beneficiary Information

Where applicable, CAPESONECCT may process 4Ps beneficiary status, household IDs, program eligibility, and outcomes. Some data may qualify as sensitive personal information under Philippine law, receiving heightened protection under the DPA.

6. Employer Information

Includes organization details (business name, classification, workforce size, tax ID), representative information (owner name, designation, contact info), and verification documents (DTI/SEC registration, business permits, government IDs).

7. Job Posting & Application Information

Job postings contain titles, requirements, salaries, and statuses. Job applications track applicant records, statuses (Pending, Reviewed, For interview, Hired, Rejected), and resumes restricted by role-based access.

8. Job Offers, Referrals, Saved Jobs & Matching

The system tracks referrals, offers, and uses algorithmic or machine-learning techniques to assist in job matching based on skills, preferences, and qualifications. A matching result is not a guarantee of employment.

9. Beneficiary Program Information

Supports programs including SPES, GIP, TUPAD, and DILP, tracking names, compensation, program timelines, and completion/absorption outcomes.

10. Administrative, Audit & Technical Information

Maintains user activity logs, failed login protections, IP addresses, session states, and authentication data necessary for system security and prevention of unauthorized activities.

11. How We Use & Share Personal Information

Processed for employment services, program administration, user verification, account security, communications, and legal compliance. Third-party processors (like Cloudinary for media storage and SMTP for emails) are utilized under strict contractual safeguards.

12. Security Safeguards & Document Protection

Protections include password hashing, role-based access control, request throttling, CSRF protection, and secure document storage. Users are strictly prohibited from downloading or disclosing another person's documents without lawful authorization.

13. Data Retention & Secure Disposal

Data is retained only as long as necessary for declared operational purposes and disposed of securely to prevent unauthorized recovery.

14. Your Rights as a Data Subject

Under the Data Privacy Act, you have the Right to be Informed, Right to Access, Right to Correction/Rectification, Right to Object, Right to Erasure or Blocking, Right to Data Portability, Right to File a Complaint with the National Privacy Commission, and Right to Damages.

15. Contact Information

For privacy questions, requests, or complaints, contact your designated Data Protection Officer or Privacy Office through the official organization email and address.

Terms of Use

1. Introduction

Welcome to CAPESCONNECT. CAPESCONNECT is an employment and workforce assistance platform operated by PESO. The Platform facilitates employment-related services, job searching, applicant registration, employer verification, job postings, applications, referrals, and workforce programs.

2. Eligibility

CAPESCONNECT is intended for individuals, employers, organizations, and authorized personnel legally permitted to use the services. By creating an account, you represent and warrant accurate information, authorization to provide it, adherence to security credentials, and lawful use.

3. Purpose of CAPESCONNECT

Facilitates applicant profile management, job searching, application tracking, saved jobs, employer verification, job management, employment referrals, workforce program beneficiary administration, resume uploads, email verification, account security, and job matching.

4. User Accounts

Requires accurate account creation information, password protection, and confidentiality. Users must not share passwords, use another's account, create fraudulent accounts, or provide false details. CAPESCONNECT may suspend or terminate accounts for violations.

5. Email Verification

Email verification confirms address ownership, protects accounts, reduces fraudulent registrations, and enables platform communications.

6. Applicant Responsibilities

Applicants must submit truthful personal details, contact info, educational background, skills, work experience, resumes, and identification credentials. Submitting false credentials or fraudulent documents is strictly prohibited.

7. Employer Responsibilities

Employers must provide accurate organization data, valid verification documents, and truthful job listings. Employers must not post fraudulent vacancies, misleading conditions, discriminatory requirements, or demand unlawful recruitment fees.

8. Employer Verification

Employers undergo verification processes reviewing business permits and identification documents, classified as pending, verified, or rejected. Verification does not constitute a legal guarantee or endorsement.

9. Job Postings

Employers may publish job titles, descriptions, qualifications, locations, and compensation. CAPESCONNECT may remove or restrict postings that violate terms or laws.

10. Job Applications

Submitting applications does not guarantee interviews, responses, employment offers, specific salaries, or outcomes. Employment decisions remain the sole responsibility of the employer.

11. Saved Jobs

Provides a convenience feature for applicants to bookmark opportunities without constituting a formal job application.

12. Job Matching

Utilizes automated systems and algorithms to analyze skills, education, experience, and preferences to suggest relevant job opportunities. Matching scores do not guarantee hiring or suitability.

13. Automated Processing

Automated processing supports, rather than replaces, human employment decisions in accordance with data privacy laws.

14. User-Submitted Content

Users remain responsible for their submitted resumes, photos, and materials, warranting accuracy and lawful ownership rights.
