Program and Services of PESO Carigara

Empowering Carigaranons through employment, training, and livelihood opportunities

SPES (Special Program for Employment of Students)

Provides short-term work opportunities during summer or school breaks

Skills Training and Development

Offers workshops to improve employability and entrepreneurship skills.

Career Guidance and Counseling

Paid internships for fresh graduates in government agencies.

Barangay TUPAD

Emergency employment for displaced workers in affected communities.

Livelihood Support and Entrepreneurship

Skills and livelihood training for unemployed and underemployed workers.

Labor Market Information (LMI) Services

• Collects and disseminates data on labor supply and demand.
• Publishes job trends and employment statistics for Carigara and nearby areas.
