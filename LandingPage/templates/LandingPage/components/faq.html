About the Public Employment Service Office

Everything you need to know about PESO — its mandate, objectives, functions, and the clients it serves.

What is the PESO?
The Public Employment Service Office (PESO) is a non-fee charging multi-employment service facility provider or entity established in all Local Government Units (LGUs) in coordination with the Department of Labor and Employment (DOLE).
What are the objectives of the PESO?
  • Ensure prompt, timely, and efficient delivery of employment services.
  • Provide relevant information on DOLE programs and services.
  • Serve as a venue for job seekers and employers to connect seamlessly.
What are the functions of the PESO?
  • Encourage employers to submit lists of vacancies for job matching.
  • Conduct career guidance, counseling, and employment coaching.
  • Facilitate local and overseas job placements and recruitment activities.
  • Provide information on various livelihood and skill training programs.
Who are the PESO clients?
  • Job seekers and displaced workers
  • Students and out-of-school youth
  • Registered employers and business establishments
  • Persons with disabilities (PWDs) and senior citizens
