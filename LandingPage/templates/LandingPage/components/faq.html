About the Public Employment Service Office

Everything you need to know about PESO — its legal mandate, core objectives, primary functions, and the communities it serves.

What is the PESO?
The Public Employment Service Office (PESO) is a non-fee charging multi-employment service facility entity established in all Local Government Units (LGUs) in coordination with the Department of Labor and Employment (DOLE). It ensures full employment and equality of employment opportunities for all.
What are the objectives of the PESO?
  • Ensure prompt, timely, and efficient delivery of employment services.
  • Provide relevant information on DOLE programs and services.
  • Serve as a central venue for job seekers and employers to connect seamlessly.
What are the functions of the PESO?
Job Matching

Encourage employers to submit vacancy lists for automated matching.

Career Guidance

Conduct career counseling, guidance, and employment coaching.

Placement

Facilitate local and overseas job placement and recruitment events.

Skills & Livelihood

Provide information on technical skill training and livelihood programs.

Who are the PESO clients?
Job Seekers & Displaced Workers
Students & Out-of-School Youth
Registered Employers & Businesses
PWDs & Senior Citizens
