Welcome to CAPESONECCT

CAPESONECCT is an employment, workforce, and social-service information platform intended to connect jobseekers and employers and to support authorized personnel in managing employment-related programs, referrals, beneficiary information, activities, and related administrative services.

By creating an account, accessing, or using CAPESONECCT, you agree to comply with these Terms and Conditions. If you do not agree with these Terms, you should not use the System.

01 Purpose of the System

CAPESONECCT is designed to support activities including, but not limited to:

Applicant & Jobseeker registration and profiling
Employer registration and verification
Job posting and management
Job applications and application-status tracking
Job referrals and employment offers
Saving and managing preferred job opportunities
Applicant-to-job matching and recommendations
PESO employment programs and activities
Beneficiary registration and program monitoring
Career guidance and related events
Authorized MSWDO institutional functions
Administrative reporting and monitoring

Feature availability may vary based on account role and authorization level.

02 User Accounts

CAPESONECCT supports distinct access tiers, including: Applicant, Employer, PESO personnel, MSWDO personnel, Administrator, and other designated institutional roles.

You are responsible for maintaining accurate and up-to-date information. You must:

  • Provide truthful and accurate credentials and keep account records updated.
  • Safeguard your password and login credentials; do not share your account.
  • Promptly report any suspected unauthorized activity and operate the system solely for lawful purposes.

03 Applicant Responsibilities

Applicants provide credentials, education history, and supporting data to compile an employment profile. You warrant that all submitted details are authentic and legally belong to you.

Applicants are strictly prohibited from:

  • • Uploading fraudulent or falsified files or credentials.
  • • Submitting another individual's profile data without proper clearance.
  • • Uploading malicious software, scripts, or misrepresenting professional experience.

04 Employer Responsibilities

Employers must maintain accurate establishment profiles and valid operational credentials. Verification documents (such as business permits or institutional licenses) may be required prior to activating recruitment features.

Employer data must be utilized strictly for legitimate hiring purposes. Unlawful redistribution, commercial data scraping, or discriminatory recruitment practices are prohibited.

05 Job Postings & Matching

Employers retain sole responsibility for the legality and accuracy of active job listings. Automated candidate matching functions as an assistive administrative tool and does not guarantee employment placement or formal hiring outcomes.

06 Beneficiary & Social-Service Programs

The platform logs program participation, tracking criteria, and administrative metrics. Data access is strictly restricted to authorized municipal personnel for institutional auditing and statutory reporting.

07 Prohibited Activities & Data Privacy

Fraud, unauthorized system tampering, mass data extraction, and security circumvention are strictly prohibited. System governance adheres to applicable legal frameworks, including the Data Privacy Act of 2012.
