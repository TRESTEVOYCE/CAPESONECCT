Commitment to Your Privacy

CAPESCONNECT respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. Operated by PESO, the Platform provides employment, workforce, job-matching, employer verification, employment referral, and related services.

This Privacy Policy operates consistently with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173), its implementing rules and regulations, and applicable issuances of the National Privacy Commission of the Philippines.

02 Personal Information We Collect

Depending on the services you use, CAPESCONNECT collects distinct categories of personal information:

Account Information

Username, email address, protected password credentials, account role, verification status, and security metrics. Passwords are securely hashed and never stored as plaintext.

Applicant Information

Full name, date of birth, sex, contact details, address, education, skills, work experience, resumes/CVs, photos, and ID documents protected under statutory safeguards.

Employer Information

Business name, address, contact details, authorized representatives, business permits, registration documents, and job vacancy details.

Applications & Programs

Application histories, job bookmarks, placement statuses, and authorized beneficiary or workforce program management records.

Technical & Security Information

IP addresses, browser type, operating system, device details, authentication logs, and security audit traces to protect platform integrity.

03 Why We Process Personal Information

Personal information is processed for legitimate operational purposes, including:

Account Management: Authentication, verification, and user services.
Employment Services: Job postings, referrals, and candidate matching.
Employer Verification: Reviewing permits to ensure jobseeker protection.
Job Matching: Facilitating candidate-employer connections.
Security & Safeguards: Fraud detection, auditing, and system monitoring.
Legal Compliance: Adhering to government mandates and statutory regulations.

04 Lawful Basis & Consent

CAPESCONNECT processes personal information based on applicable lawful bases under Philippine law, including explicit consent, contract performance, legal obligations, and statutory government mandates.

Where consent is required, it is gathered in a freely given, specific, informed, and demonstrable manner. You may withdraw consent subject to legal limitations and statutory retention requirements.

05 Information Sharing & Disclosure

Personal information is disclosed strictly on a need-to-know basis:

  • With Employers: Applicant profiles and resumes are shared with registered employers upon application.
  • With Applicants: Job details and public employer profiles are viewable by registered jobseekers.
  • Authorized Personnel: Municipal PESO/MSWDO administrators access data strictly for official duty.
  • Third-Party Service Providers: Secure cloud and email infrastructure bound by privacy agreements.
  • Legal Obligations: Disclosures mandated by court orders or statutory government requests.

CAPESCONNECT does not sell users' personal information.

06 Data Security & Retention

We implement robust technical, organizational, and physical safeguards—including password hashing, role-based access control, secure HTTPS communication, and operational audit logging—to protect your personal information.

Data is retained only as long as necessary for service fulfillment, legal compliance, and institutional archiving policies. Outdated records are securely deleted or anonymized.

07 Your Data Subject Rights

Under the Data Privacy Act of 2012, you hold fundamental privacy rights:

Right to be Informed Understand how your data is collected and processed.
Right to Access Request copies of your personal information records.
Right to Rectify Correct inaccurate or outdated personal details.
Right to Erasure / Object Request blocking or deletion of data where appropriate.
Data Portability Obtain personal data in a structured, accessible format.
Right to Lodge Complaint File grievances with the National Privacy Commission (NPC).
