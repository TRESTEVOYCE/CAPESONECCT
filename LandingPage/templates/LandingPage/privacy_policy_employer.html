02 Personal Information We Collect

Depending on the services you use, CAPESCONNECT collects distinct categories of personal information:

Account Information

Username, email address, password credentials in protected form, account role, account status, email verification status, and security-related information. Passwords are stored securely using appropriate hashing mechanisms and are never stored as plaintext.

Applicant Information

Full name, date of birth, sex, mobile/telephone number, address, educational background, skills, work experience, employment history, employment preferences, preferred work locations/types, resume, curriculum vitae, profile photograph, identification documents, and other profile details. Certain details may qualify as sensitive personal information under Philippine law, protected by strict safeguards.

Employer Information

Employer or business name, address, contact info, representative details, business registration information, permits, supporting documents, verification records, and job vacancy details necessary for recruitment.

Job Applications, Saved Jobs & Programs

Application history, job status, saved job records, and where applicable, authorized beneficiary and workforce program management data.

Technical & Security Information

IP address, browser type, operating system, device details, login timestamps, authentication events, session data, and security audit logs used to safeguard system integrity.

03 Why We Process Personal Information

Personal information is processed for legitimate purposes, including:

  • Account Management: Authentication, email verification, and role-specific services.
  • Employment Services: Job postings, applications, referrals, and workforce support.
  • Employer Verification: Reviewing permits and credentials to protect jobseekers.
  • Job Matching: Assisting candidates and employers in finding relevant opportunities.
  • Security & Maintenance: Fraud prevention, auditing, and threat mitigation.
  • Legal Compliance: Meeting government mandates and regulatory requirements.

04 Lawful Basis & Consent

CAPESCONNECT processes personal information based on applicable lawful bases under Philippine law, including consent, contract performance, legal obligations, and statutory government mandates.

Where consent is required, it is gathered in a freely given, specific, informed, and demonstrable manner. You may withdraw consent subject to legal limitations and ongoing statutory retention requirements.

05 Information Sharing & Disclosure

Personal information is disclosed strictly on a need-to-know basis:

  • With Employers: Applicant profiles, resumes, and contact info are shared with authorized employers when applying for jobs.
  • With Applicants: Job details and public employer descriptions are accessible to jobseekers.
  • Authorized Personnel: Municipal PESO/MSWDO staff and administrators access data strictly for legitimate operational duties.
  • Third-Party Service Providers: Trusted cloud hosting, email delivery, and infrastructure providers bound by strict privacy requirements.
  • Legal Disclosures: When mandated by court orders, lawful government processes, or the Data Privacy Act.

CAPESCONNECT does not sell users' personal information.

06 Data Security & Retention

We implement robust technical, organizational, and physical safeguards—including password hashing, role-based access control, secure HTTPS sessions, and audit logging—to protect your data.

Data is retained only as long as necessary for fulfillment of services, compliance with legal obligations, and institutional archiving policies. Once no longer required, records are securely deleted or anonymized.

07 Your Data Subject Rights

Under the Data Privacy Act of 2012, you possess fundamental rights regarding your personal data:

Right to be Informed Know how your data is collected and processed.
Right to Access Request access to your personal information records.
Right to Correct Rectify inaccurate or outdated personal details.
Right to Object / Erasure Object to processing or request deletion/blocking.
Data Portability Obtain your data in a structured, machine-readable format.
Right to File Complaint Lodge complaints with the National Privacy Commission (NPC).
